## Topic

Identifying the First Customer and Core Value Proposition

## Decision #001: Primary Customer

Project Spector will initially target:

- Paranormal YouTubers
- Paranormal content creators
- Paranormal television productions

These customers produce large amounts of video, audio, and investigative evidence and have a stronger financial incentive to improve review and production workflows than hobby investigators.

## Problem Statement

Paranormal content creators often collect evidence from multiple sources during an investigation, including:

- Cameras
- Body cameras
- Audio recorders
- EMF meters
- Environmental sensors
- Investigator notes

Reviewing and synchronizing this evidence is time-consuming and frequently requires manually searching through hours of recordings.

## Founder Insight #002

The primary challenge is not collecting evidence.

The primary challenge is organizing, reviewing, and correlating evidence efficiently after an investigation.

## Core Value Proposition

Project Spector helps paranormal creators find important moments faster.

The platform centralizes investigation evidence into a single case file and presents synchronized events through a unified timeline.

## Why Existing Tools Are Not Enough

Current paranormal equipment manufacturers focus primarily on collecting data.

Most investigators must manually organize recordings, notes, and sensor data using multiple applications and storage locations.

Project Spector aims to become the central platform where all investigation evidence is organized, reviewed, and analyzed.

## Initial MVP Direction

Version 1 will focus on:

- Case management
- Evidence uploads
- Timeline synchronization
- Event markers
- Investigation notes

Advanced AI analysis and hardware integrations will be considered after validating the core workflow.
