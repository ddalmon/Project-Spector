## Project Name

Project Spector

## Problem Statement

Paranormal investigators often collect evidence from multiple devices including audio recorders, video cameras, EMF meters, environmental sensors, and investigator notes.

Evidence is frequently stored across different devices and formats, making review time-consuming and difficult to synchronize.

## Founder Insight #001

Paranormal investigators do not primarily need more equipment. They need a better way to organize, synchronize, review, and understand the evidence they already collect.

## Initial Product Vision

Project Spector will serve as a centralized evidence management platform for paranormal investigations.

Core capabilities:

- Evidence organization
- Timestamp synchronization
- Investigation timelines
- Case management
- Cross-device event correlation
