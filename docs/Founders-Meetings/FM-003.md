# Founders Meeting 003

Date: August 17, 2026

## What is Project Spector?

Project Spector is a case management and media analysis platform for paranormal productions that automatically organizes, timestamps, and correlates investigation evidence into a unified timeline.

## Who is the First Customer?

Project Spector's initial target customers are:

- Paranormal YouTube creators
- Paranormal content production teams
- Paranormal television productions

These customers create large amounts of investigation footage and have a strong need to reduce review and editing time.

## What Problem Costs Them the Most Time?

The most expensive and time-consuming part of a paranormal investigation is reviewing evidence after the investigation.

Production teams often spend countless hours reviewing:

- Video footage
- Audio recordings
- Investigator notes
- Sensor readings

Project Spector aims to reduce the time required to locate, organize, and analyze important investigation events.

## Founder Insight #003

The value of Project Spector is not helping investigators collect more evidence.

The value is helping them find meaningful evidence faster.
